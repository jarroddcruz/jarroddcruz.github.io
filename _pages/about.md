ᜋᜊᜓᜑᜌ᜔! (<i>mabuhay!</i>)

I am M.A. student in the [Department of Linguistics](https://lin.ufl.edu/) at the University of Florida. I am interested in how we can connect Indigenous language communities with statistical, computational, and linguistic specialists in ways that promote revitalization and community-driven research. Consequently, my areas of academic interest include language documentation and revitalization, endangered language lexicography, and applied linguistics in general.

As a researcher in the [Machine Learning for Endangered Language Documentation (MELD) Lab](https://meld.lin.ufl.edu/) @ UF, I have worked on developing the [Digital Language Consultancy](https://meld.lin.ufl.edu/research/digital-language-consultancy/) which provides data services for Indigenous language community projects. This work connects students in linguistics and programming with language communities, facilitating service learning, future collaboration, and language resource creation.

As a Filipino-American, I am also interested in the languages of the Philippines, including my heritage languages Tagalog and Ilokano. I hope too that, directly or indirectly, my work will support the revival of the many languages of the Philippines.
